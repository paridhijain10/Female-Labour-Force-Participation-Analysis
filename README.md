# Female Labour Force Participation: Cross-Country Econometric Analysis

## Overview

A cross-country econometric analysis examining the determinants of female labour force participation (FLFP) across 80 non-high-income countries using 2022 World Bank World Development Indicators data.

## Research Question

What demographic, structural, health, and regional factors are associated with female labour force participation in non-high-income economies?

## Methodology

- Cross-sectional Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) regression
- Sample: 80 non-high-income countries
- Year: 2022
- Data Source: World Bank World Development Indicators
- Significance level: 5%

## Variables

### Dependent Variable
- Female Labour Force Participation Rate (FLFP)

### Independent Variables
- Urban Population (%)
- Log(Fertility Rate)
- Log(Female Life Expectancy)
- South Asia Dummy

## Econometric Model

FLFP = β₀ + β₁(Urban Population) + β₂(log Fertility) + β₃(log Female Life Expectancy) + β₄(South Asia Dummy) + ε

## Key Results

- R²: 0.3223
- Adjusted R²: 0.2861
- F-statistic: 8.916
- Observations: 80
- All four independent variables were statistically significant at the 5% level.
- White's test indicated no evidence of heteroskedasticity.
- VIF results indicated no serious multicollinearity.
